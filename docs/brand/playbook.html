01 — Brand at a Glance

Everything PawLocal is, in one spread

Positioning

For Mumbai's pet-owning households, PawLocal is the hyperlocal pet services marketplace that connects them directly to verified vets, groomers, walkers and trainers via WhatsApp — with zero booking fees and zero middlemen — because we believe the best pet care already exists in your neighbourhood, it just needs a trusted front door.

Brand Voice in Three Words

Warm. Trusted. Local.
Never corporate. Never clinical. Always the knowledgeable neighbour.

The Bet

Mumbai has 800,000+ pets and a pet care market growing at 15% CAGR. Every national platform — Supertails, Wiggles, Sploot — either sells products or manages the service. Nobody owns the discovery + trust layer. PawLocal does.

The inDrive Parallel

inDrive disrupted Ola/Uber not by running a fleet but by letting supply and demand negotiate directly. PawLocal does the same for pet services: verified providers, WhatsApp-direct contact, zero commission. The platform value is trust and discoverability, not service delivery.

The Moat

Reviews + verification data compound. Every approved listing, every review, every Pet Broadcast interaction creates a trust layer that is genuinely hard to replicate fast. A new entrant can build a directory; they cannot build 500 verified reviews overnight.

Primary Markets

Juhu (live) → Andheri West → Bandra → Santacruz. Dense residential pockets with high pet ownership and WhatsApp-native households.

03 — Typography System

Two fonts. A clear hierarchy.

DM Serif
DM Serif Display — Display & Headlines only Why: Serifs signal earned authority and warmth simultaneously. DM Serif in particular has a soft, rounded letterform that feels approachable rather than stiff — important for a brand built on neighbourhood trust. Competitors like Sploot use geometric sans; DM Serif is our differentiator.

Usage: Hero H1, H2 section headings, pull quotes, stat numbers. Never for body copy, labels, or buttons.

Sizes: Hero: clamp(2.5rem, 5vw, 5rem) · H2: 2.25rem · H3: 1.5rem · Pull quote: 1.25rem
Inter
Body, labels, UI
Inter — Body, UI, labels, buttons Why: Inter is the gold standard for legibility on screens. At small sizes (10–12px for category labels and badges) it remains readable — critical for mobile where we need to show provider name, rating, price, distance and category in a small card. Alternatives like Poppins or Nunito feel rounder but sacrifice information density.

Size scale: Body 16px · Body-sm 14px · Label 12px · Caption 11px · Tiny badge 10px

Weights: Regular (400) for body · Medium (500) for labels · Semibold (600) for card titles · Bold (700) for CTAs and stats

04 — The PawLocal Framework

The Paw Promise — 5 non-negotiables

Every brand that wins long-term has operational non-negotiables that compound into a moat. These are PawLocal's five. Violate any one and the entire trust proposition unravels.

🔍 01 — Verified First
Why non-negotiable

Every provider goes live only after human review. This is what separates PawLocal from JustDial, Sulekha, and Google Maps. Those platforms list anyone. PawLocal lists people your neighbour would recommend.

How to embed

✓ Verified badge on every card — prominent, not buried. "Reviewed by PawLocal team" tooltip. Show review date.

The risk of staying quiet: One bad experience from an unverified-looking listing will circulate on 10 Juhu WhatsApp groups before you wake up. Mumbai operates on social proof. A single "I found a fraud on PawLocal" post undoes six months of goodwill. Verification is not a feature — it's your insurance policy.
💬 02 — WhatsApp Native
Why non-negotiable

Mumbai runs on WhatsApp. 97% of Indian smartphone users use it daily. Every booking platform that built its own chat lost to providers who figured out "just message on WhatsApp." PawLocal doesn't fight this — it makes WhatsApp the product.

How to embed

✓ Every CTA ends in WhatsApp. Pre-filled message template. Provider phone visible on profile. Zero friction to contact.

The risk: Build a custom in-app chat and you'll spend ₹15L building something providers never check. Your conversion rate will halve overnight the moment a user realises they have to use an inbox nobody monitors.
₹0 03 — Zero Commission
Why non-negotiable

Urban Company takes 20–35% from providers. That margin comes from somewhere — either higher prices to consumers or lower income to providers. PawLocal's zero-fee model creates genuine loyalty from providers that no commission platform can match. A provider earning full income through PawLocal will never delist.

How to embed

✓ "₹0 fee, always" in every hero, every provider pitch. Make it a badge of honour, not a footnote.

The risk: The moment providers suspect hidden fees are coming, they list on competitor platforms "just in case." Provider trust, once lost in a B2B2C model, is gone permanently. Be fanatically transparent on this forever.
📍 04 — Genuinely Local
Why non-negotiable

Supertails is a national brand that ships to your door. Wiggles is veterinary advice for India. PawLocal is specifically for Juhu. Then Andheri. Then Bandra. The neighbourhood specificity IS the product — a groomer 0.5km away beats a 4.9-star one 8km away, always.

How to embed

✓ Show distance in km, not just area name. "Near you" language everywhere. Area-specific taglines per category page.

The risk: Expand too fast and the "hyperlocal" claim becomes hollow. A Mumbai directory with 3 providers per area feels emptier than a Juhu directory with 30. Depth before breadth, always.
📣 05 — Demand Visible
Why non-negotiable

The Pet Broadcast feature flips the model. Instead of only supply (providers) being visible, demand (pet owners who need a service now) is visible too. This is PawLocal's structural differentiator. No competitor — Sploot, JustDial, Sulekha — has this. It creates a real-time signal of market health that compounds with every broadcast posted.

How to embed

✓ Broadcast count on homepage. "X active requests near you" for providers. Make demand visible in both directions.

The risk: If the Broadcast section sits quietly in the nav and nobody uses it, PawLocal is just another directory. The inDrive model only works when both sides know the other side is active. Broadcast volume is a growth metric, not a feature metric.

09 — CRO Roadmap · What's costing conversions today

Five fixes. Each one measurable.

Observation 1 — Header has no location context on mobile

The "📍 Juhu, Mumbai" location pill is hidden on mobile (hidden sm:flex). Mobile users — PawLocal's primary audience — land on a generic page with no local signal.

Estimated cost: 15–20% of mobile users bounce within 3 seconds because the page doesn't feel local to them. That's the only reason they came.

Fix: Show a small location chip on mobile too. Use the OnboardingSheet area to personalise: "Pet care in Juhu" → "Pet care in Bandra" once they set their area.

Observation 2 — Category pages show 0 providers for dog walking

Dog walking is listed prominently but has zero approved providers (they're being onboarded manually). A visitor who taps Dog Walking sees an empty state — possibly the service they came for.

Estimated cost: Every empty category visit has a 70%+ bounce rate and a WhatsApp group message that says "PawLocal has nothing for walkers."

Fix: Replace empty state with a "We're onboarding walkers — be first to know" email/WhatsApp capture. Turn a dead end into a lead.

Observation 3 — Pet Broadcast has no social proof of activity

The /broadcast page exists but shows "No active requests right now" when empty. This signals low usage to early visitors who are evaluating whether to trust the platform.

Estimated cost: The inDrive model only creates trust when both sides see the other side is active. An empty broadcast feed kills the network effect before it starts.

Fix: Seed 3–5 real broadcasts manually. Even "test" requests from team members create the appearance of a live marketplace to early visitors.

Observation 4 — /join form is long with no progress indicator

The provider registration form has 15+ fields on one page. No step indicator. No "saved" state. A walker filling this out on mobile will abandon at field 8.

Estimated cost: Every 1 additional form field reduces completion by ~3–5% on mobile. At 15 fields vs. a 3-step wizard, you're losing 30–40% of provider signups.

Fix: Break into 3 steps: Basic info → Location & availability → Photos & bio. Show "Step 1 of 3." Auto-save draft to localStorage.

Observation 5 — No urgency signal for emergency vet search

The single highest-intent visit on PawLocal is someone searching for an emergency vet at 10pm. The current vet category page shows all vets in the same visual weight — emergency and non-emergency listed together.

Estimated cost: This is the moment that either earns a lifelong loyal user or sends them back to Google. You will be judged on this one experience more than any other.

Fix: Pin emergency vets to the top of the list with a distinct red clay card. "Open now · 24hr · 0.8km" — the three things they need at 10pm, immediately visible.
