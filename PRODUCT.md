# PupStep — Product Context

## Product Purpose
GPS walk proof app for Indian dog parents. Walker logs pee/poop/route on a live map. Parent receives a WhatsApp report card after every walk. Core wedge: health-aware proof of walk, not a marketplace.

## Register
product

## Users
- **Pet Parent** — pays, urban Indian, 25–45, Mumbai. Anxious about dog's health. Wants proof every walk happened. WhatsApp-first. Mobile only.
- **Dog Walker** — maid, watchman, family friend. Low tech literacy. Hindi/Marathi speaker. Token-based access, no account. Uses the app during the walk.
- **Admin** — internal (melroy@verfolia.com). Sees MRR, pipeline, report quality.

## Brand Tone
Warm, reassuring, practical. Not cute-overload. Not clinical. "Your dog went for a walk and here's proof" energy. Mumbai local feel. Paw prints, not puppies.

## Anti-References
- No Wag/Rover marketplace vibes
- No generic pet app pastel overload
- No SaaS dashboard coldness
- No heavy animations for their own sake

## Strategic Principles
- Every screen moves parent closer to: receive first report
- Walker screens optimised for zero-friction: big buttons, Hindi labels
- Report card is the hero product — it's a postcard, not a dashboard
- Payment comes after the parent feels the value, not before
